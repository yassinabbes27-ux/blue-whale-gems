THE STORIES WITHIN

Every stone has a story.

A journey through the origins, history and timeless beauty of natural gemstones.

ANCIENT GREECE & EGYPT

Amethyst

Admired for its violet colour, amethyst appeared in ancient jewellery and treasured objects across the Mediterranean world.

ANCIENT CIVILISATIONS

Lapis Lazuli

For thousands of years, this deep-blue stone travelled along trade routes and was prized in royal ornaments, carvings and ancient art.

MILLIONS OF YEARS IN THE MAKING

Amber

Amber begins as tree resin. Over millions of years, it can harden into a warm golden material that sometimes preserves traces of ancient life.

A GIFT FROM THE SEA

Natural Coral

Formed by tiny marine animals over generations, coral has been shaped into adornment and treasured across many coastal cultures.
